What's inside

Everything a seafarer carries, in one place

Seafarer's card

Your details on a card with a QR code. A colleague scans it with the phone camera and saves you as a contact.

Documents & reminders

Scan a certificate with the camera — numbers and dates are read for you. Reminders come well before anything expires.

Voyage & crew

Current vessel, days on board and to sign-off, crew list with birthdays, position history and links to MarineTraffic and VesselFinder.

Logbook

Notes, expenses, handover points and anything worth remembering from each contract — searchable and tagged.

Sea time & CV

Total sea service and experience by vessel type, a service record and an international CV in PDF — ready for any crewing agency.

Handover

Pass your notes and crew list to your reliever as one file, and take over theirs the same way.

Privacy

Your data never leaves your phone

Sea Wolf has no servers and no accounts. Everything you enter is stored only on your device, so it works in the middle of the ocean and nobody else can read it.

  • No registration, no cloud, no tracking
  • No ads and no analytics
  • PIN and fingerprint lock
  • Encrypted backup file that you keep yourself
  • Works fully offline at sea
Made at sea

Built by a seafarer for seafarers

Sea Wolf was created by a serving gas engineer who was tired of folders of paper, lost certificates and rewriting the same CV for every agency. It's free for all colleagues at sea.
